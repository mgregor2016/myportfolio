CatwalkTv.com

A logo designed for all the seasons of fashion.

I imagined a website where one could get to experience all the runway shows one could handle virtually. This would be the logo for said brainchild.

BRAND STRATEGY

The logo was designed to be versatile, a little sassy and to celebrate the young female model in an empowered pose though the site would be open to all sexes and genders that could appreciate a really good fashion show...
