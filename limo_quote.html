Everyone Wants to Ride in the Limo

Celebrating the Homegrown Wisdom of Oprah Winfrey

Inspired by a quote from one of my heroes Oprah Winfrey, I imagined her saying these words in her understated tone but with a compassionate and sage oratorical delivery. Words and letters take on a life of their own. The bigger and bolder the letter forms the more stressed and inflected I imagined them to be when she said them.

THE APPROACH

First approach combines a black and white image taken while studying in France of a rainy night along a bustling road set as the backdrop to the type of the quote. The second approach is executed using only text in grayscale. The limo literally breaks and the traffic might be interpreted with the typographical play.
