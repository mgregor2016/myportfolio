I Rise by Maya Angelou

This motion graphics piece celebrates the poetry of the late great Maya Angelou. Words that inspire next to emotive images and backed by a few beats for the soul.

When I created this piece Maya Angelou was very much alive and well but her words have always taken me some place ethereal and otherworldly. Hearing her words spoken by herself as only she could, invokes images of a strong and yet beautiful god force that ebbs and flows with rhythm and lyricism.

Artistic Direction

I wanted to create a positive piece that echoes the sentiments of legacy, fortitude and eventual spiritual flight layered in her poetry. My process is interactive: I discovered this version after revisiting the original concept at least three other times. I designed the graphics as well as the sound. I love the marriage of sound and imagery in this particular version.
